# Campus Lost & Found

Describe a lost item in your own words and get a ranked shortlist of found
items, matched against their photographs by a zero-shot OpenCLIP model that
was never trained on campus data. Ownership claims are checked against a
private detail the owner recorded, and released by a security officer.

Source code, evaluation and documentation:
https://github.com/datalordstephen/campus-lost-and-found

This demo resets whenever the Space restarts; anything you add is temporary.
